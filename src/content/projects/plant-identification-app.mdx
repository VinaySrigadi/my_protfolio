## Overview

The Plant Identification Mobile App bridges the gap between deep learning and mobile engineering by bringing a high-accuracy Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) directly to the edge. Built with Flutter and TensorFlow Lite, the application allows users to identify plant species instantly without requiring an internet connection.

## Edge AI Engineering

Deploying computer vision models in the cloud introduces latency and requires constant connectivity. For outdoor botanical identification, offline availability is critical.

- **Model Optimization**: I trained and quantized a CNN, converting it into the highly compressed `.tflite` format. This drastic reduction in model size allowed it to be bundled directly within the mobile application bundle.
- **Performance**: The optimization techniques resulted in **sub-200ms inference latency** directly on the mobile CPU/NPU.

## Results

- **Accuracy**: Achieved **85%+ classification accuracy** across 30 distinct plant species.
- **User Experience**: By using Flutter, the application provides a smooth, native-like experience on both iOS and Android. Users can point their camera at a leaf or flower and see the classification bounding boxes and probabilities update in near real-time, completely offline.

## Technologies Used
- **Frontend Framework**: Flutter (Dart)
- **Machine Learning Engine**: TensorFlow Lite
- **Model Architecture**: Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)
